# Data-Visualisation-Empowering-Business-with-Effective-Insights--Job-Simulation
Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights - TATA Job Simulation

For the CEO — business health & growth
1.	Revenue & growth trend — What's monthly/quarterly revenue over the period, and is the business growing or plateauing?
2.	Profitability proxies — Which products/categories drive the most revenue, and which are high-volume but low-value (margin risk)?
3.	Customer concentration — What % of revenue comes from the top 10–20% of customers (Pareto check)? Are we overexposed to a few large accounts?
4.	Returns/cancellations — What's the scale and cost of the ~10.6K negative-quantity (returned/cancelled) transactions, and which products or customers drive them?
5.	Geographic performance — How dependent is revenue on the UK vs. international markets, and where's the untapped opportunity?
6.	Seasonality — Are there predictable peaks (e.g., pre-Christmas) we should be planning inventory/cash flow around?
7.	Order economics — What's the average order value and how has it trended — is basket size growing?
For the CMO — customers & marketing effectiveness
1.	Customer segmentation (RFM) — Who are our most valuable customers by Recency, Frequency, Monetary value, and how should we tier them for retention campaigns?
2.	Retention vs. acquisition — What % of customers are repeat buyers vs. one-time, and what's the reorder rate/time between purchases?
3.	Churn risk — Which previously active customers have gone quiet, and are worth a win-back campaign?
4.	Best-selling & trending products — What should be featured in campaigns, and are there products with rising momentum worth promoting?
5.	Cross-sell/basket analysis — What items are frequently bought together, useful for bundling or recommendation engines?
6.	New customer acquisition trend — Are we adding new customers month over month, and at what rate relative to churn?
7.	International expansion signal — Which non-UK countries show the strongest organic demand, suggesting where a localized campaign could pay off?
